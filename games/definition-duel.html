How to Play

A split-screen rapid matching challenge!

  • Two definitions appear simultaneously on left and right sides
  • Each definition has 4 word options below it
  • You have 12 seconds per round to match BOTH definitions
  • Select one word for each definition, then click Submit
  • BOTH must be correct to score points
  • If one is wrong, no points and move to next round
  • Build a streak for consecutive perfect rounds
  • Game ends after 15 rounds or if timer runs out
