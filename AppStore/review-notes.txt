No login or account. Everything runs offline; nothing is collected.

To see the 5.2 changes:
- Play: drag the word that matches the definition into the hole at the top of the paper (or turn on "Tap words instead of dragging" under the i button and tap it). A new install starts with common words.
- Pronunciation: after a right answer the app shows what all four words mean. Tap the speaker beside a word to hear it. It uses the voices built into iOS. Tap anywhere else to go on.
- Widget: add "Word of the Day" from the Home Screen widget gallery. Its word comes from a list inside the app and changes daily.
- Larger text: set an accessibility text size in Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text. The game switches to a layout with tap answers.
- More Blanks only: under the i button, "Progress" now has words per review box and "Missed this week". Answer a word wrong once and it appears there.

The tip jar in Blanks (i button > Buy Me a Coffee) uses the three existing consumable in-app purchases; it unlocks nothing.
